Nomination rules

  1. Only colleagues in support roles can run. Sales consultants and service colleagues are not eligible.
  2. Every submission uses your @rentokil-initial.com Google account. The email comes from your sign-in and cannot be edited.
  3. You can only vote for someone on the nominee list.
  4. Each email can vote once. Votes cannot be changed or withdrawn.
  5. Colleagues who are running cannot vote, and you cannot vote for yourself.
  6. If you have already voted, you can no longer run.
  7. The nominee with the most votes is selected.
