This policy explains what information the international trip nomination site collects, why, and how it is handled. The site is run by [ORGANISER NAME / DEPARTMENT] at [COMPANY NAME] (the organisers).

What we collect

  • Your work email address and name, taken from your Google sign-in (the sign-in token is checked and not stored).
  • If you run: your department and the name you enter.
  • If you vote: the name you enter and the work email of the nominee you vote for.
  • The date and time of each submission.

We do not collect your Google password, contacts, files or any other Google account data.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm you are an eligible colleague with a work account.
  • To record nominations and votes, and to enforce the rules: one vote per person, and nominees cannot vote.
  • To work out and announce the nomination result.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Submissions are stored in a Google Sheet managed by the organisers, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Only the organisers can see who voted and for whom. Nominee names, departments and work emails are shown to signed-in colleagues so they can choose who to vote for. We do not sell your information or share it outside the company, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Records are kept until the nomination process has ended and the result is confirmed, then deleted or archived by [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 90 days].

Your choices and rights

Taking part is voluntary. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, subject to the voting rules and applicable data protection law. Votes cannot be changed once cast.
